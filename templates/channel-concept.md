## Monetization And Originality Rules

- No celebrity likeness, voice, speeches, or direct quote compilations.
- Every video must have an original thesis, not only a list of motivational lines.
- Every symbolic insert must connect to the narration.
- Every video must include Japanese subtitle adaptation, not machine-style direct translation.
- Reused templates must be customized by theme, emotion, visuals, and final mission.
- The description should explain the original writing, AI-assisted narration, Japanese subtitle adaptation, and cinematic editing process.
