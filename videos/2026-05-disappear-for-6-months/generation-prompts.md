# Video Generation Prompts

## Global Visual Rules

- Make the video feel like a cinematic self-improvement short film.
- Do not imitate a specific movie, actor, athlete, creator, or public figure.
- Keep motion subtle and realistic.
- Use simple actions that video AI can render cleanly.
- Prefer atmosphere, breath, rain, light, fog, walking, and slow camera movement.
- Avoid complex hands, fast workouts, fights, crowds, and long facial transformations.

## Character Continuity

- Main character description: Original Japanese or mixed-Asian young man, early twenties, lean build, short black hair, tired but focused eyes.
- Clothing: dark hoodie or plain black training jacket, no logos.
- Emotional baseline: exhausted but deciding to become disciplined.
- Color palette: deep teal, black, muted gray, cold blue, small warm light in final scenes.
- Continuity note: Keep him original and generic enough to avoid any celebrity resemblance.

## Reusable Motion Phrases

- Slow push-in, subtle handheld camera movement, realistic breathing.
- Slow tracking shot forward through fog, branches moving gently.
- Static close-up with rain running across the window, light flickering softly.
- Slow pan from left to right, shallow depth of field, dust in the light.
- Locked-off shot, only the subject's shoulders move with breath.
- Slow pull-back revealing the character alone in a large space.

## Kling Start/End Frame Direction

For shots that need clearer movement, use paired Start/End images instead of a single image.

Priority scenes:

- S01: forest entrance -> deeper forest
- S09: phone glowing -> phone face down
- S15: fog path -> deeper forest path
- S20: looking down in mirror -> eyes lifted in mirror
- S22: dark road -> sunrise road
