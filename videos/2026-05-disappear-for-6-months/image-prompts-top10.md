# Image Prompts Top 10

Use these prompts for ChatGPT image generation. Generate in 16:9 cinematic format. Do not include text inside the image.

## Global Instruction

```text
Create a cinematic 16:9 still image for an original motivational video.
Do not use or imitate any celebrity, athlete, actor, movie scene, brand logo, or copyrighted character.
No text, no captions, no watermark, no logos.
Realistic, cinematic, high contrast, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field where appropriate.
Color palette: black, deep teal, cold blue, muted gray, with warm light only in hopeful scenes.
```

## S01 - Forest Entrance

```text
Create a cinematic 16:9 wide shot of an original young Japanese or mixed-Asian man standing alone at the edge of a dark forest path at night. He is seen from behind, wearing a plain black hoodie with no logo. Tall trees surround the path, cold fog drifts between the trunks, and the wet ground reflects faint blue-green light. The mood is quiet, private, and serious, like someone choosing to disappear from the world for a while. Deep teal and black color grade, high contrast, subtle film grain, realistic atmosphere, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```
