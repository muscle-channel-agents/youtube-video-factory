# Video Script

## Metadata

- Working title: Disappear for 6 Months
- Target length: 3:45-4:30
- Voice: Low male narrator, calm pressure, older-brother tone.
- Emotional arc: quiet confrontation -> decision -> isolation -> work -> return.
- BGM arc: dark pulse -> slow rise -> emotional restraint -> silence on final line.

## Structure

| Chapter | Time | Purpose | Emotional Mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0:00-0:12 | Stop the scroll | Quiet command |
| Problem | 0:12-0:55 | Name the overexplaining habit | Direct, uncomfortable |
| Decision | 0:55-1:35 | Choose silence and protection | Calm intensity |
| Work | 1:35-3:10 | Show the hidden process | Persistent, cinematic |
| Return | 3:10-4:00 | Show future evidence | Controlled confidence |
| Final Mission | 4:00-4:25 | Give one action | Quiet force |

## English Speech

```text
Disappear for a while.

Not because you are weak.
Not because you lost.
But because the next version of you needs silence.

Stop telling people what you are about to do.
Stop explaining the plan.
Stop asking for belief from people who have never protected a dream of their own.

Your goal is still young.
Do not hand it to people who only know how to doubt.

For the next six months, become harder to reach.
Not rude.
Not bitter.
Just focused.

Turn the phone over.
Leave the group chat quiet.
Let them wonder where you went.

You do not need an audience for the beginning.
You need a room.
A notebook.
A calendar.
A body that keeps showing up when your mind wants comfort.

There will be days when nothing changes.
No applause.
No message.
No sign that it is working.

That is the middle.
That is where most people go back to their old life.

You will not.

You will walk through the fog.
You will take the hard road.
You will train when nobody is recording.
You will study when nobody is clapping.
You will build when nobody understands.

And slowly, quietly, something will happen.

Your promises will start to trust you again.
Your mornings will become cleaner.
Your eyes will become calmer.
Your work will become evidence.

Six months from now, you do not need to be famous.
You do not need to shock the world.
You only need to look in the mirror and know:

I did not abandon myself this time.

So today, do one thing in silence.
One thing you have been avoiding.
Do it without posting.
Do it without explaining.
Do it because your future self is watching.

Build quiet.
Return different.
```

## Voice Direction

- Hook: Whisper-level calm, slow, with space between lines.
- Problem: Slightly sharper, as if calling out a habit.
- Decision: Controlled and grounded.
- Work: More rhythmic, steady pressure.
- Return: Warm but not soft.
- Final Mission: Drop the music slightly, speak like a private instruction.
