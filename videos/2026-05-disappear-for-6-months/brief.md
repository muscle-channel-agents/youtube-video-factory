## Positioning

- One-sentence concept: A cinematic speech that tells the viewer to stop explaining, disappear from distractions, and quietly rebuild for six months.
- Viewer state before watching: Distracted, overexplaining goals, tired of starting over, hungry for a reset.
- Viewer state after watching: Calm, focused, and ready to do one private action today.
- Why this video is not a generic motivation clip: It uses one clear thesis, a six-month silence framework, symbolic forest and road inserts, and a concrete end mission.
- Original point of view: Your next version does not need attention. It needs protection.
- Repeated channel phrase to include: Build quiet. Return different.

## Viewer

- Primary viewer: Japanese male viewer in his late teens to early thirties who likes English motivation and wants to rebuild discipline.
- Current frustration: He keeps announcing plans, scrolling, comparing, and losing momentum.
- Hidden desire: He wants to become unrecognizable without having to convince anyone first.
- Trigger emotion: "I do not need witnesses. I need discipline."
- Reason to save or rewatch: Use before studying, training, working, or deleting distractions.

## Core Message

- Main promise: Six months of quiet, focused work can change how you see yourself.
- Main tension: Attention feels good now, but it weakens the work before it grows.
- Hard truth: Nobody can protect your dream if you keep handing it to people who do not understand it.
- Hopeful payoff: One day, you will not need to explain. The evidence will speak.
- Final line: Build quiet. Return different.

## Visual Direction

- Dominant mood: Dark, private, cinematic, disciplined.
- Color palette: black, deep teal, cold blue, muted gray, small warm sunrise accents.
- Main location motifs: dark bedroom, glowing phone, forest path, empty road, desk at night, early morning street, gym, calendar.
- Symbolic inserts: lone figure entering forest, foggy path, rain on window, road at sunrise, door closing.
- Avoided visuals: celebrities, public figures, luxury flexing, random gym montage, fast action, recognizable movie scenes.
- Thumbnail emotion: intense face close-up or lone figure in darkness with simple text "GO GHOST".

## Production Notes

- Main voice style: Low, controlled, strict but supportive. Start quiet, build pressure, end calm.
- BGM direction: Minimal dark pulse at start, slow rise in the middle, restrained cinematic release at the end.
- Pace: 3-5 second visual cuts, slower during symbolic inserts, pauses before key lines.
- Risk notes: Avoid copying any famous speech cadence too closely. No celebrity likeness. No quote compilation structure.
- Assets to create: 18-24 AI video clips or image-to-video clips, 8-12 static motion/text cuts, narration MP3, BGM, thumbnail image.
