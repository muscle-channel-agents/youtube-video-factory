# Audience Persona

## Primary Persona

- Name: Ren
- Age: 21
- Gender: Male
- Location: Japan
- Occupation or stage: University student or early-career worker.
- English level: Understands simple English phrases and likes the sound of English motivation.
- YouTube viewing habit: Watches at night, before studying, before the gym, or when feeling behind.
- Device: Smartphone with headphones.

## Current Life State

- What he is trying to change: His body, discipline, grades, work output, confidence, or side project.
- What he keeps failing to continue: Training, studying, posting, building, waking up early.
- What he is embarrassed to admit: He talks about goals more than he acts on them.
- What distracts him: Phone, friends, comparison, late-night scrolling, explaining plans.
- Who does not understand him: Friends who joke about effort, family who sees only current results, people who think his goals are unrealistic.
- What he wants people to notice later: That he became different without announcing the process.

## Emotional Triggers

- Words that wake him up: disappear, lock in, no excuses, do it quietly, results speak.
- Situations that hit him: Being alone at night, deleting distractions, walking through rain, waking before others.
- Visuals that feel like his life: Dark room, glowing phone, empty road, gym mirror, forest path, calendar marks.
- Fears: Staying the same, being laughed at, wasting his twenties, regretting not trying.
- Desires: To become undeniable, calm, disciplined, respected, and self-trusting.
- Hidden pride: He wants to be the person who did the work when nobody saw it.

## Purchase Or Subscribe Reason

- Why he watches: To switch from scrolling mode to action mode.
- Why he subscribes: The channel feels like a ritual before work, training, or study.
- Why he rewatches: Certain lines make him feel accountable.
- Why he comments: To declare a quiet commitment without explaining everything.
- Why he shares: To send the mood to a friend who is also trying to change.

## Language Design

- English phrases he understands quickly: I can do this, lock in, do the work, don't quit, go ghost, keep going.
- English phrases that feel cool but still clear: Build in silence, return different, no announcement, just evidence.
- Japanese subtitle tone: Short, natural, direct, emotional.
- Japanese words to use: 黙って, 積み上げろ, 消えろ, 集中しろ, 証明しろ, 戻ってこい.
- Japanese words to avoid: 成功確定, 誰でも, 爆伸び, 絶対稼げる, 楽勝.

## Content Fit

- Best themes: Ghost Mode, Discipline, Start Again, You vs You.
- Weak themes: Luxury flexing, hustle without purpose, aggressive money claims, generic positivity.
- Best thumbnail text: GO GHOST, LOCK IN, I CAN DO THIS, DON'T QUIT, BUILD SILENT.
- Best opening lines: "Disappear for a while." / "Stop telling people what you are about to do." / "You are not lazy. You are distracted."
- Best final missions: Turn off your phone for one hour, do one avoided task, train without posting, wake up before your usual time.

## Secondary Persona

- Name: Kai
- Age: 29
- Difference from primary persona: Already working full time and wants to rebuild discipline after burnout.
- Why this person may still watch: The tone feels mature enough and not purely teenage hype.
- What not to over-optimize for: Too much slang, too much gym-only imagery, or overly aggressive alpha-male framing.
